Association review

Review the moments immediately before, during, and after seven interrupted histories in five development clips. These are selected failures, not a representative benchmark.

Markers are torso centers, not skeletons. P is the archived primary observation; M0–M3 are raw multi-pose slots. Their numbers do not identify people. Overlapping markers alone cannot prove duplicate poses. Use uncertain whenever the image is insufficient.
How to label

Count all visibly recognizable people, including partly occluded people. Assign person-1, person-2, etc. consistently within each clip, starting left to right in its first shown frame. Multiple observations assigned to the same actor indicate a suspected duplicate. Describe clothing and position in notes so another reviewer can check your identity assignments. Mark non-person only with visible supporting evidence; explain uncertain and non-person labels. Counts start blank, not zero. No labels are saved automatically: export a draft before closing.

Single-review exports record one reviewer’s judgments. They do not establish ground truth, independent adjudication, fall recall, or permission to promote a detector. Research media stays local; do not publish this packet.
